# Bias checklist (run before generating ideas)

Answer each line in one sentence inside the report. A "yes" on any of the first
four means go back one step.

1. **Headline-first?** Did any thesis start from a single news story rather
   than from at least three items in different scan sections?
2. **Missing counter-evidence?** Does any thesis have fewer than two
   counter-points?
3. **Recency weight?** Is more than half of the evidence for a thesis from the
   last 48 hours? If so, look for the longer trend that it sits on, or label
   the thesis tactical.
4. **Portfolio steering?** Did the user's existing positions shape which
   theses you picked? (Positions are read after the scan for that reason.)
5. **Priced in?** For each thesis: what does the market already price, and
   what would have to happen for it to move further?
6. **Crowding?** Is this the consensus trade of the week? If yes, say so and
   lower confidence by one.
7. **Narrative fit vs. data?** Would you hold the same thesis if the most
   memorable quote behind it turned out to be wrong?
8. **Previous ideas.** Check the scorecard (`references/journal-handoff.md`):
   are you repeating an idea type that has recently lost? Say so.
